Mobile & Browser Audio Troubleshooting:
  • iPhone / iPad: Ensure the physical Silent Switch (side switch) is turned OFF so browser chimes can play.
  • Background Lock Screen Alarms: Mobile browsers restrict audio when phone screens are locked. Use "Export to Phone Calendar" to get system lock-screen alarms.
  • Volume: Check device media volume level.

Real-Time Sync Guide

Dual-Engine Real-Time Sync connects your Phone, iPad, and Laptop without passwords or accounts.

How to Pair Devices:
  1. Tap "Create New Code" on your main device.
  2. Copy the generated 6-digit Sync Code (e.g. SPOT-8492).
  3. Enter this code into the Sync box on your second device and tap Pair Devices.
  4. Any checkmarks marked on your phone now update live on your laptop screen!
